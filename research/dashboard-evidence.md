# GHO dashboards, current rates, and forward economics

Observation date: **2026-09-05**. Browser-visible data, not crawler freshness. No claim that all pages share one block timestamp. All monetary observations are displayed rounded values.

## Key finding

TokenLogic DOES publish a GHO financial dashboard at https://aave.tokenlogic.xyz/gho/financials . Its definition is broader than the user's borrow-interest-only numerator and is not an independently reconciled complete economic P&L. Its headline is positive, but this cannot establish profitability under the user's formula.

At observation, the dashboard's 90-day annualized header displayed revenue **$8.970M**, expenses **$6.748M**, net **+$2.221M**. Components of annualized revenue were Core **$3.75M**, Prime **$923.23K**, Horizon **$1.08M**, GSM **$3.22M**. GSM is **36%** of revenue and comprises backing deposit yield, swap fees, and incentive yield. Source for every value: https://aave.tokenlogic.xyz/gho/financials , observed 2026-09-05.

Subtracting GSM from the rounded headline gives **~$5.750M/year** non-GSM facilitator revenue and **~-$0.998M/year** after the same dashboard expenses (8.970-3.220-6.748). This is **a partial sensitivity calculation, not the requested strict net income**: non-GSM facilitator revenue itself can include both borrow interest and DAO facilitator deposit yield; expense classification requires reconciliation; the requested rate-discount opportunity cost and other missing costs are not individually shown. Using reported net less GSM gives -$0.999M from rounding. Prefer presenting ~-$1.00M. The parent agent has a separate quarterly statement extraction that disagrees with this chart in at least one quarter.

The dashboard also displays lifetime total revenue **$24.92M** and total expenses **$25.16M**, a rounded cumulative deficit of **~$0.24M** under that dashboard's own categories. These must not be relabelled user-defined net income. Same URL/date.

## Exact expense methodology inspected

The info button beside **GHO Expenses / Accrual basis** on /gho/financials opens methodology distinct from the main DAO statements. Paraphrase:

- Direct GHO programme payments from Aave-controlled wallets are combined with rewards accrued to GHO Safety Module stakers.
- The Safety Module expense accrues from configured daily AAVE token emissions, valued using each day's AAVE price, even if rewards remain unclaimed.
- GHO programme token transfers to liquidity venues or exchanges are recognized in the transfer period. Categories include liquidity incentives and exchange-listing spend.
- Daily expenses aggregate into monthly and quarterly figures; daily token prices are used.
- The methodology does not establish that liquidity/CEX transfers exclude recoverable principal; it does not explicitly give the user's discounted-rate opportunity cost, a reconciled actual Merit distribution ledger, or GHO bad debt/GSM operating-cost ledger.

The methodology text explicitly says it was generated by an AI model. That is a reason to reconcile implementation, not an automatic reason to discard data.

The separate **Annualised Expenses (90D)** info dialog says past 90-day expenses scaled to a year, but later refers to the most recent month and compares days 30–60. Treat the headline as _labelled 90D_ and flag the documentation inconsistency; do not silently repair it.

## Forward cost warning

Source: https://aave.tokenlogic.xyz/gho/financials , observed 2026-09-05.

The live sGHO card shows **162.32M GHO**, savings **4.50%**, last-30-day sGHO spending **$453.43K**, and a trailing annualized cost **$5.52M/year**. GHO CEX shows last-30-day **$123.51K**, or **$1.50M/year**. Overall last-30-day expense is **$583.9K**, annualized **$7.105M**. GHO Umbrella and discretionary liquidity cards display **$0** last-30-day spending, but this is not proof of zero forward authorized cost; their YTD figures and individual claim transactions are nonzero.

At the current displayed balance and savings APR, a simple one-year constant-balance illustration is **$7.3044M** (=162.32M×4.50%). That exceeds the historical **$5.52M** savings run-rate. Adding the historical **$1.50M** CEX run-rate gives **~$8.80M/year** as a mixed current/trailing scenario, NOT a verified complete current burn. Using the official savings balance gives **$7.2738M** (=161.64M×4.50%). Use one balance/source consistently and label this as an illustrative fixed-balance APR cost, not a guaranteed compounded annual return. Complete forward net cannot be proven because cost execution, facilitator capture and definitions remain unreconciled.

## Confidence and final framing

- High transcription confidence: official current rate/cap values, TokenLogic visible labels, Dune timestamp fields, August governance table.
- Medium economic confidence: dashboard categories and annualized values are provider-reported; expense documentation and timeframe labels have inconsistencies.
- Low completeness confidence: user-defined net P&L, full Merit paid-out attribution, subsidy opportunity-cost series, recoverable liquidity principal exclusion, realized GHO loss reconciliation, GSM operating cost attribution.
- The narrow question remains unproven from this branch's evidence. A broader dashboard surplus and a negative GSM-excluded partial scenario coexist because they use different revenue definitions and incomplete expense reconciliation. Do not label either as a verified strict net P&L.
