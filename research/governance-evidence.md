## Accounting boundaries

- A budget/allowance is authorization or treasury transfer, not proof of quarterly expense recognition or rewards ultimately claimed. Do not spread a multimonth authorization evenly over quarters.
- GHO-denominated amounts below are token units unless source explicitly gives USD. A historical GHO reward should be valued using actual payment/expense-date USD price, especially pre-peg; do not silently call every unit $1.
- Native stkAAVE discount is foregone interest, already absent from interest actually accrued/received. For the user's requested additional opportunity-cost subtraction, show actual gross interest and separate discount opportunity cost; explain that this is an economic adjustment beyond realized cash income. Do not treat potential standard-rate revenue as actual revenue.
- Umbrella coverage rewards are GHO-specific incentive costs for the GHO module; they are not a borrower discount. Do not allocate all protocol-wide Umbrella spending to GHO.
- New native sGHO savings costs replace the fixed Merit budget; do not add the old full $14.3M/year Merit run-rate to new sGHO ASR costs.
- Earmarked GHO inventory/principal, collateral loans, CRV/BAL voting assets and protocol-owned liquidity principal are not automatically operating expense. Need actual expense or loss data.
